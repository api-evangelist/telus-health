# TELUS Health

> TELUS Health is the digital-health division of TELUS, one of Canada's largest telecommunications and technology companies, and the country's leading health-IT provider (PS Suite, Med Access, and the cloud-native Collaborative Health Record / CHR EMRs, plus pharmacy, virtual care, and employer/benefits health services). Its documented public integration surface is the CHR Enterprise API — a GraphQL API secured with RS512-signed JWT bearer tokens — complemented by the TELUS Patient Chart FHIR R4 implementation guide (89 StructureDefinition profiles). Both surfaces are documented but gated behind a partner or CHR-domain agreement.

## APIs
- [TELUS CHR Enterprise API](https://help.inputhealth.com/en/articles/6483215-chr-enterprise-api): GraphQL API over the Collaborative Health Record (patients, appointments, encounters, referrals, tasks, clinical documents). Query = 64 fields, Mutation = 49 fields. Cursor Connections pagination (default 50, max 100). Auth: RS512 JWT bearer, tokens expire within 15 minutes; endpoint provisioned per CHR domain.
- [TELUS Patient Chart FHIR API](https://simplifier.net/teluspatientchart): FHIR R4 (4.0.1) implementation guide — 89 StructureDefinition profiles/extensions under the http://telus.com/fhir/patientChart canonical.
